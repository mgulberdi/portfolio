Patient 360 & Agentforce

Healthcare provider platform
Most recent build
Health CloudData CloudAgentforce ApexLWCFHIRMuleSoft

One patient, one record — with an AI agent that actually knows them.

The problem. Patient data lived in the EHR, the billing system, and a patchwork of engagement tools. Care coordinators toggled between five screens to answer one phone call, and no system could see the whole patient.

The build. I implemented Health Cloud person accounts as the single patient record, with Data Cloud identity resolution stitching EHR, billing, and engagement identities into one unified profile. Clinical, lab, pharmacy, and telephony data flows through an API-led integration layer, and an Agentforce service agent handles routine patient questions — appointment lookups, care-plan status, coverage checks — grounded in the patient's actual record, with clean escalation to a human coordinator.

Real-time bidirectional sync

Fintech engagement
Integration architecture
Change Data CapturePub/Sub APIKafka Platform EventsREST

Two systems writing to the same records — without stepping on each other.

The problem. A fintech company ran Salesforce alongside a homegrown internal platform. Both wrote to CRM records, neither knew the other existed, and updates flowed one way on inconsistent schedules. The requirement: real-time, bidirectional sync — a change in either system reflected in the other immediately.

The design. An event-driven integration hub sits between the two. Salesforce publishes changes via Change Data Capture over the Pub/Sub API; the internal platform publishes through an outbox pattern into Kafka. A canonical data model translates both directions, while echo suppression stops updates from ping-ponging, idempotency keys make retries safe, and last-writer-wins with field-level rules resolves genuine conflicts. Where CDC doesn't cover an object, a tiered fallback kicks in: Platform Events fired from Apex, then watermark-based polling as the floor.

Events in both directions, translated once, delivered exactly once in effect.
Real-timeseconds-level propagation replacing inconsistent batch schedules

3-tierfallback chain: CDC → Platform Events → watermark polling

Order Management

National retail clothing brand
Consulting engagement
Salesforce OMSFlow orchestrationApex Commerce APIsPayments

From "where's my order?" to a fulfillment engine the brand could trust.

The problem. A national clothing brand's order lifecycle was split across its ecommerce storefront, a warehouse system, and manual service workarounds. Split shipments, returns, and exchanges routinely broke, and service agents had no reliable view of an order's true state.

The build. I implemented Salesforce Order Management as the system of record for the post-purchase lifecycle: order ingestion from the storefront, fulfillment orchestration flows routing to the right warehouse, payment capture on shipment, and a full returns-and-exchanges process with automated refunds. Service agents got a single order timeline inside the console, so "where's my order?" became a ten-second answer instead of a three-system hunt.

One lifecycle engine between storefront and warehouse — and one timeline for the agents answering the phone.
1 timelinesingle source of truth for every order's lifecycle in the service console
Automatedreturns, exchanges, and refund flows that previously required manual intervention
Peak-readyorchestration built and load-tested for holiday-season order volume

Financial Services Cloud

Financial services firm
Consulting engagement
FSCExperience CloudMarketing Cloud ApexLWC

Giving advisors the household view their spreadsheets never could.

The problem. Advisors at a financial services firm tracked client relationships in disconnected tools — one client's accounts here, their spouse's there, the family trust nowhere. Cross-sell opportunities and life-event triggers slipped through the gaps.

The build. I delivered a Financial Services Cloud rollout centered on householding: relationship groups tying individuals, joint accounts, trusts, and businesses into one financial picture, with financial accounts and rollups surfaced on every profile. Action plans standardized onboarding and review workflows, Marketing Cloud journeys fired on life events, and an Experience Cloud portal gave clients self-service access to their own picture — securely scoped to exactly what they should see.

Every relationship rolls up to the household — and the household powers the console, the portal, and the journeys.
Household-levelrollups replacing per-person views scattered across tools
